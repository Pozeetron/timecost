## Full description

TimeCost turns money into time.

Enter your earnings rate and any purchase price — instantly see how many hours, days, weeks, or months of work it really costs.

**Why TimeCost**
• Understand spending in the only currency that matters: your time  
• Flexible rates: per minute, hour, day, or week  
• Customize work units (hours/day, days/week)  
• Dozens of currencies  
• Clean, light interface  
• Automatic language based on your device (20 popular languages, English fallback)  
• Your rate stays on your device  

**How it works**
1. Choose your currency  
2. Enter how much you earn  
3. Type a purchase price  
4. See the time cost instantly  

Make clearer buying decisions. Download TimeCost.
